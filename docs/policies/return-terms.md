# Return Terms — Example Electronics Store (fictional, PoC sample)

> Sample policy for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC. The company is fictional. Rules follow the Polish Act of 30 May 2014 on consumer rights (consolidated text Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1796) as read on 2026-11-25. Have a lawyer review it before any production use.
>
> The decision agent cites rules by their ID (for example `R-2.1`).

## R-1 Scope

- **R-1.1** These terms cover returns of goods bought by consumers from Example Electronics Store, both online and in our physical stores.
- **R-1.2** Online and other distance purchases: the right of withdrawal follows from art. 27 of the Consumer Rights Act.
- **R-1.3** In-store purchases: Example Electronics Store voluntarily offers the same return terms. There is no statutory obligation.
- **R-1.4** A return does not require a reason. If the customer gives a reason, it is used only to improve service.

## R-2 Return period

- **R-2.1** The customer may return goods within **14 calendar days** from the day they received them.
- **R-2.2** For this preliminary assessment, the date of purchase entered in the form counts as the day of receipt.
- **R-2.3** If the return is up to 7 days past the 14-day period, the case goes to manual review, because delivery may have happened after the purchase date.
- **R-2.4** A return more than 7 days past the 14-day period is preliminarily rejected. If the product is faulty, the customer may still file a complaint (see the Complaint Terms).

## R-3 Condition of the returned goods

- **R-3.1** The customer may handle the goods the way they would in a shop, to check their nature, features and operation.
- **R-3.2** If the goods show no signs of use beyond that (no scratches, dents, stains or wear, and screen protective films still in place where present), the return is accepted with a **full refund**.
- **R-3.3** If the goods show signs of use beyond what was needed to check them (scratches, scuffs, wear, dirt, missing protective films, physical damage), the return is still accepted. The refund is reduced by the diminished value of the goods (art. 34 ust. 4). The service team sets the exact deduction after inspection.
- **R-3.4** If the goods show physical damage that makes them unsellable as new or refurbished (cracked screen, broken housing, liquid damage), the case goes to manual review.
- **R-3.5** Goods should be returned complete, with accessories and, where possible, the original packaging. Missing items may increase the deduction under R-3.3.

## R-4 Exclusions

- **R-4.1** Products delivered in a sealed package that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons (for example in-ear headphones sold in a sealed hygiene package) cannot be returned if the seal was opened after delivery (art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 5).
- **R-4.2** Software or recordings delivered in a sealed package cannot be returned if the seal was opened after delivery (art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 9).
- **R-4.3** Products made to the customer's own specification cannot be returned (art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 3).

## R-5 Refund

- **R-5.1** The refund is paid within 14 days from receiving the customer's return statement (art. 32 ust. 1). Example Electronics Store may hold the refund until the goods arrive or the customer shows proof that they were sent.
- **R-5.2** The customer sends the goods back within 14 days from the return statement (art. 34 ust. 1).

## R-6 Next steps for the customer

- **R-6.1** Fill in the return statement on the store website or at a store.
- **R-6.2** Pack the product with its accessories and send it to, or drop it off at, an Example Electronics Store service point.
- **R-6.3** The service team inspects the product and confirms the final decision and the refund amount.
