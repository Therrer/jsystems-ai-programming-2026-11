# Complaint Terms — Example Electronics Store (fictional, PoC sample)

> Sample policy for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC. The company is fictional. Rules follow the Polish Act of 30 May 2014 on consumer rights, chapter 5a (consolidated text Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1796), as read on 2026-11-25. Have a lawyer review it before any production use.
>
> The decision agent cites rules by their ID (for example `C-2.1`).

## C-1 Scope

- **C-1.1** These terms cover complaints about goods that do not conform to the contract of sale: defects and malfunctions present at delivery (art. 43b, 43c).
- **C-1.2** A manufacturer's warranty (gwarancja) is a separate, voluntary procedure that these terms do not cover. Its terms are in the warranty card.
- **C-1.3** Only Example Electronics Store's service team issues the formal answer to a complaint, within 14 days of receiving it (art. 7a). An automated preliminary assessment is not that answer.

## C-2 Liability period

- **C-2.1** Example Electronics Store is liable for non-conformity that exists at delivery and appears within **2 years** of delivery (art. 43c ust. 1).
- **C-2.2** Non-conformity that appears within 2 years of delivery is presumed to have existed at delivery, unless proven otherwise (art. 43c ust. 1).
- **C-2.3** For this preliminary assessment, the date of purchase entered in the form counts as the delivery date.
- **C-2.4** If the complaint is up to 7 days past the 2-year period, the case goes to manual review.
- **C-2.5** A complaint more than 7 days past the 2-year period is preliminarily rejected under these terms. The customer is pointed to the manufacturer's warranty (C-1.2) or a paid repair.

## C-3 Assessing the cause

- **C-3.1** **Covered (likely non-conformity):** defects with no sign of external cause, such as dead pixels, display lines without impact marks, battery swelling without physical damage, faulty buttons or ports with no sign of force, manufacturing defects (gaps, loose parts), and malfunctions described by the customer that the photo does not contradict.
- **C-3.2** **Not covered (likely external cause):** damage from impact or a fall (cracked glass with an impact point, dents, bent housing), liquid damage (corrosion, stains), burns or melting from external heat, and damage from unauthorized repair or opening.
- **C-3.3** **Normal wear** (light scratches from everyday use, worn coating) is not non-conformity in itself, unless it appears much earlier than expected for this type of product.
- **C-3.4** **Unclear cause:** if the photo and the description do not let us tell a defect from external damage, or they contradict each other, the case goes to manual review. The presumption in C-2.2 favours the customer.
- **C-3.5** The customer's description is a valid source of facts for malfunctions that a photo cannot show (for example "does not charge" or "restarts by itself").

## C-4 Remedies

- **C-4.1** First remedy: **repair or replacement**. The customer may choose, and the store may offer the other option if the chosen one is impossible or would cost excessively (art. 43d).
- **C-4.2** **Price reduction or refund (withdrawal)** is available when the store refused or failed to repair or replace the product, when the defect persists after repair, or when the defect is serious enough to justify it straight away (art. 43e ust. 1). Non-conformity is presumed to be significant (art. 43e ust. 4).
- **C-4.3** Default suggestion for a first complaint: repair. Suggest replacement when repair is unlikely to be possible (for example a product with no repairable parts) or when the customer reports the same defect after an earlier repair. Suggest a refund only in the C-4.2 cases.
- **C-4.4** Not covered damage (C-3.2): the store may offer a **paid repair** after the service team gives a quote.

## C-5 Next steps for the customer

- **C-5.1** File the formal complaint on the store website or at a store, with proof of purchase and a description of the defect.
- **C-5.2** Deliver the product to an Example Electronics Store service point. The store covers transport costs for complaints.
- **C-5.3** The service team inspects the product and sends the formal answer within 14 days of receiving the complaint. If it does not answer in time, the complaint is deemed accepted (art. 7a ust. 2).
- **C-5.4** A customer may file a formal complaint even if the preliminary assessment was negative.
